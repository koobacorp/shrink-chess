# Shrink Chess (prototype)

Fast chess vs a bot where the board crumbles: the outer ring falls after move 12 (8×8 → 6×6) and the next after move 22 (→ 4×4). Pieces on falling squares are lost; if your king falls you lose. 5 s per move (8 s on Easy) or a random move is played. No mate by move 32 → most material wins.

Single-file, mobile-first: open `index.html`. Play: https://koobacorp.github.io/shrink-chess/
